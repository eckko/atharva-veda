# Glossary: fixed renderings for the Atharvaveda layers

Read at the start of each chunk. The reviewer extends it; the drafter does not.

- deva = god (देव); devatā = deity; ṛṣi = sage (ऋषि); mantra = mantra/verse (मन्त्र)
- Divine and personal names: transliterate, no translation (Indra, Agni, Soma, Varuṇa, Atharvan, Aṅgiras); English without diacritics dropped, in IAST (Varuṇa)
- brahman (n.) = sacred utterance/prayer (ब्रह्म), Brahmā (m.) = priest/deity as the context shows; give the alternative in notes
- yakṣma = wasting disease (यक्ष्म); do not identify a modern disease
- Plant, drug and substance names: keep the name, gloss "plant name" unless transparent; never identify a species or state a medical effect
- rakṣas, piśāca, yātudhāna = rakṣas, piśāca, yātudhāna (kinds of harmful beings): keep the word with the gloss "harmful being"
- svāhā = svāhā (the offering call); vaṣaṭ = vaṣaṭ
- Hindi names and terms in Devanagari; no Latin letters in the Hindi
