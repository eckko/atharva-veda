001.034.2a | At the tip of the tongue be honey for me, at the root of the tongue madhūlaka. May you be only in my purpose; you come to my mind.
001.034.2b | ~
001.034.3a | Honeyed be my going out, honeyed my going away. With speech I speak sweetly; may I be of honey-like look.
001.034.3b | ~
001.034.4a | I am sweeter than honey, more honeyed than the honey-yielder. You shall surely love only me, like a honeyed branch.
001.034.4b | ~
001.034.5a | I have gone around you with an encircling sugar-cane, for freedom from hatred, so that you may be loving toward me, so that you may not be one going away from me.
001.034.5b | ~
001.035.1a | When the Dākṣāyaṇas, kindly-minded, bound on gold for Śatānīka, that I bind on you for life, for splendour, for strength, for long life and for a hundred autumns.
001.035.1b | ~
001.035.2a | Neither rakṣasas nor piśācas overpower him, for this is the first-born vigour of the gods. He who wears the Dākṣāyaṇa gold makes for himself a long life among the living.
001.035.2b | ~
001.035.3a | The splendour, light, vigour and strength of the waters, and the energies of the trees: like Indra, we set powers upon this one; let him, growing strong, wear that gold.
001.035.3b | ~
001.035.4a | With the seasons of the years and months, and with the milk of the year, we (I) fill you; may Indra and Agni and all the gods, not being angry, give their assent to you.
